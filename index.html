About Our Farm

Our Story

Established in 2010, Faizi Wildlife Farm has grown to become one of Pakistan's most reputable exotic animal breeding facilities. Located in the lush countryside of Punjab, our farm spans over 20 acres of carefully designed habitats that mimic the natural environments of our animals.

We specialize in breeding rare and exotic species with a focus on animal welfare, conservation, and responsible ownership. Our team of experienced zoologists and veterinarians ensure that all animals receive the highest standard of care.

Our Mission

To promote conservation through responsible breeding and education.

Our Values

Animal welfare comes first in all our operations.
